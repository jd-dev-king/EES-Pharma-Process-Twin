## Automation status

The PLC & Automation Center remains a planned Sprint 3.0 module. Sprint 2.7 displays only read-only alarms and interlock signals already emitted by the existing Mixing, Packaging, and CIP simulations. It does not claim to provide PLC rack visualization, ladder/FBD monitoring, I/O forcing, or manual/automatic equipment control.
